**TARGET:** Parents, caregivers, family members

### [EXHIBIT 1 EXAMPLE - 45 seconds]

"Let's say they're playing the Light Bulb exhibit.

Three switches. One lamp. They figure out which switch controls it.

Seems simple. But here's what's actually happening:

They're learning the scientific method:
1. They form a hypothesis ('I think it's switch A')
2. They design a test (flip the switch)
3. They observe the result (lamp on/off)
4. They conclude (A definitely controls it)

This is how scientists think. This is how they should think.

When they later struggle with hypothesis-testing in science class, remind them: 'Remember the light bulb? Same thing.'"

---

### [THE CALIBRATION MOMENT - 45 seconds]

"Here's a more interesting one: the Calibration Booth.

They make predictions and rate their confidence.

If they're right at high confidence: great, keep that.

If they're wrong at high confidence: *this is the learning moment.*

They realize they don't actually know what they think they know.

This is huge. Most adults never learn this. Your kid is learning it at age 12.

What you'll notice: They get quieter. More thoughtful. They start asking 'How confident am I really?'

That's wisdom. That's maturity. That's what we hope intelligence becomes."

## GENERAL VIDEO TIPS

### For All Scripts:
- **Open hook first (5 seconds):** Capture attention before explanation
- **Show gameplay:** Long enough that viewer sees the discovery
- **Slow-motion on aha moments:** Make the learning visible
- **Text overlays:** Emphasize key concepts
- **Quick cuts during explanation:** Keep pace fast
- **Return to gameplay:** Show impact of what was explained
- **Clear call-to-action:** What does viewer do next?

### Pacing:
- **Intro:** 10-15% of runtime
- **Mystery/Setup:** 20-30%
- **Discovery:** 30-40%
- **Insight/Application:** 15-25%
- **Call-to-action/Outro:** 5%

### Tone:
- Genuine enthusiasm (this is cool, not salesy)
- Respect for viewer intelligence
- Concrete examples over abstractions
- Wonder and curiosity as model

### Technical:
- Good Minecraft visibility (zoom on important blocks)
- Audio clear (Redstone sounds add authenticity but shouldn't drown narration)
- Captions (some viewers watch muted)
- Transitions clean (dissolves better than cuts for educational content)
