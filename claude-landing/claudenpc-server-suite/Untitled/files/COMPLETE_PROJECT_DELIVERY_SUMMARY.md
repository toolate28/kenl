## What Persists

From the Information Architecture synthesis, we identified principles that persist beyond changing techniques:

1. **Information Theory** - The universe runs on information. Understanding how systems route, compress, and use information is foundational.

2. **Constraint Reveals Structure** - When you force something into a constrained system, the essence reveals itself. Redstone constraint reveals computational principle.

3. **Explicit Representation Prevents Hallucination** - Systems that represent their uncertainty explicitly don't confabulate. This applies to circuits, AI, and humans.

4. **Test-Time Thinking Substitutes for Parameters** - You don't need more raw power if you can think harder at runtime. The Reroller proves this: strategic thinking beats reckless action.

5. **Information Architecture Enables Coordination** - Systems that make information asymmetry visible (like Redstone circuits) can coordinate better than those that hide it.

These principles will be true for 100 years. They'll underpin education, AI development, and human coordination far into the future.

The Safe Spiral Redstone Museum embeds these principles into discoverable form. Kids won't remember "information theory." They'll remember the aha moments when they realized how systems work.

That lasts forever.
