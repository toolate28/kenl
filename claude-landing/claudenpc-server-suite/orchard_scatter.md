## ORCHARD GROWTH PATTERNS

**Seeding:** New project starts in one constellation
**Sprouting:** Connects to a laneway (shared infrastructure)
**Branching:** Touches adjacent constellation (tangent opportunity)
**Fruiting:** Produces exportable patterns
**Scattering:** Patterns spread to other projects (Chinese whispers)

## CHINESE WHISPERS EFFECT

Each person who receives an export:
1. **Plants** in their own orchard (adapts patterns to their domain)
2. **Cultivates** based on their expertise (adds clarity, removes noise)  
3. **Harvests** improved patterns (better than original)
4. **Scatters** again (passes enhanced version forward)

Information enriches through relay. Orchards improve through sharing.
